sugid (Cebuano) — to tell, to report.

What it does

One shared system for information that used to travel separately.

Announcements for the right audience

Barangays, municipal departments and approved organizations publish notices, advisories and events. Each one is addressed to a specific barangay or to everyone. Residents can search, filter and bookmark them.

Concerns that reach an office, and stay trackable

Residents report public problems like roads, water or trash to the office that should handle them, then follow the status. Private complaints use a separate, confidential form.

Staff tools scoped by role

Barangay officials, departments, organizations and LGU IT each see only what their role allows. Firestore and Storage security rules enforce those limits too, not just the screens.

How a concern works

From a resident's phone to the office that can act on it.

  1. Choose who should handle it.A barangay, an LGU department, or an approved organization.
  2. Describe it.Add details, photos and the location. Unfinished drafts stay on the phone.
  3. Staff work on it.The receiving office sees it in its queue and updates the status.
  4. Follow it to the end.The resident sees each change, and neighbors can see public reports on the Barangay Board.
Active In Progress Resolved If staff need more details, the concern waits as Awaiting Information until the resident replies.

Everyday services

Everything else a resident needs, in the same app.

  • Emergency Center

    Active alerts, safety status and one-tap hotlines, including 911 and local agencies.

  • Bayanihan Works

    Neighbors post help requests and offer to help, then coordinate in messages.

  • Local Market

    Local produce and goods with prices; buyers tap “I'm interested”. No payments in the app.

  • Community Calendar

    Upcoming activities, with event announcements added to the calendar.

  • Map & Explore Barili

    Barangay map, destinations such as Mantayupan Falls, and directions.

  • Barili Weather

    Current conditions and an hourly forecast.

  • Officials & organizations

    Municipal officials, your barangay's page, and local organizations.

  • E-SUGID Assistant

    Answers common questions and opens the right screen. It guides, it doesn't decide.

For staff

A web dashboard for the people who answer.

  • PublishNotices, advisories and events, now or scheduled, each with its audience.
  • RespondConcerns by status and category; complaints in their own queue.
  • AlertA three-step emergency alert: type, message, then audience and schedule.
  • Verify & recordResident verification, team accounts, reports and system logs.

Made for Barili

Built around 42 barangays.

Every announcement, concern and alert belongs to a place. E-sugid uses Barili's barangay boundaries, so a barangay can speak to its own residents and the municipality can reach all of them.

Boundaries: Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) 2020 administrative layer, as included in the app. Hover a barangay for its name and area.
